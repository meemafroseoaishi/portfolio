About Me

I am an MS student in Biotechnology and Genetic Engineering with an academic background in Agriculture. I currently work as a Research Assistant at Gazipur Agricultural University, where I am involved in research on plant genetics, genomics, and crop improvement.

I am particularly interested in understanding the genetic basis of important agronomic and seed-related traits so that this knowledge can contribute to better crops.
